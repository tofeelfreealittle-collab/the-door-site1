The World

A Wound In The Ground

Long ago, something split the planet almost in two. What's left is a crater vast enough to hold seven kingdoms along its broken rim — and, hanging in the sky at its center, a small sun of porcelain and light.


They call it home. She has never been there. She only remembers falling from it.

The Vessel

Not Quite A Child.
Not Quite A Weapon.

White dress. White face. A skeleton of pale metal built around an engine of pure light — and limbs that aren't metal at all, but shadow, soft and obedient where the rest of her is not.


She does not bleed. She dims. And somewhere beneath the ash, something is counting how much light she has left.

Part One

The Seventh Kingdom Stands. Barely.

Six kingdoms fell to weapons that don't kill — they unmake. A shadow struck true doesn't die; it collapses into something soft, wet, and wrong, still twitching with the memory of a mind.


The seventh is the last. She fell into a house that had just been emptied this way. No one answered when she knocked. No one was left to.
